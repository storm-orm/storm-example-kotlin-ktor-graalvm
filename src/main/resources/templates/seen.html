Bring your history from IMDb. On IMDb, open Your ratings or one of your lists, choose Export, and download the CSV file from Your account › Exports. Upload it here: every movie in it is marked as seen on the day you rated it, or added it to the list, and that day replaces the one here for movies you already marked. A rating of 8 or more becomes a thumbs up, 5 or less a thumbs down, unless you gave one here.
